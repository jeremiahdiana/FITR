2. Brand Account & Subscription

2.1 Account Creation. To sell on FITR, you must create a brand account and provide accurate, complete information including your legal business name, contact email, and billing details. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials.

2.2 Subscription Fee. Access to the FITR brand seller dashboard and product listing capabilities requires a subscription of $19.00 USD per month ("Subscription Fee"). The Subscription Fee is charged automatically at the start of each billing cycle via Stripe.

2.3 Auto-Renewal. Your subscription automatically renews monthly unless you cancel before the renewal date. You authorize FITR to charge your payment method on file for each renewal period.

2.4 Cancellation. You may cancel your subscription at any time by emailing app@joinfitr.com from your account email address, or by managing your subscription through your brand dashboard. Upon cancellation, your account and listings remain active until the end of the current paid billing period. No refunds are issued for partial billing periods.

2.5 One Account Per Brand. Each brand may maintain only one active brand account. Creating multiple accounts to circumvent policies or fees is prohibited and may result in immediate termination of all associated accounts.

2.6 Account Accuracy. You agree to keep your account information (business name, email, shipping address, banking details) current and accurate at all times.

3. Commission & Payouts

3.1 Platform Commission. FITR charges a 12% commission on the gross sale price (excluding shipping) of each item sold through the platform. You retain 88% of each sale's product revenue.

3.2 Commission Calculation. Commission is calculated on the final selling price paid by the customer, before shipping costs. Discounts, promotions, or coupons applied to a sale reduce the base amount on which commission is calculated.

3.3 Payout Schedule. Payouts are processed every two (2) weeks via Stripe to your registered bank account or debit card. Minimum payout threshold is $10.00 USD. Amounts below the threshold roll over to the next payout cycle.

3.4 Payout Delays. FITR reserves the right to hold payouts for up to 14 additional days in cases of suspected fraud, high dispute rates, or pending investigations. We will notify you if a hold is placed on your account.

3.5 Taxes. You are solely responsible for determining and paying all applicable taxes on your sales, including sales tax, VAT, and income tax. FITR may issue a 1099-K if your annual sales exceed IRS reporting thresholds. You agree to provide accurate tax identification information upon request.

4. Product Listings

4.1 Accuracy. All product listings must be accurate, complete, and not misleading. This includes product names, descriptions, ingredients, weight, dimensions, photos, and pricing. You are solely responsible for the accuracy of your listings.

4.2 Pricing. You set your own product prices. FITR may suggest pricing based on market data but the final price is always your decision. Prices must be in USD and must reflect the actual price customers will pay (excluding shipping).

4.3 Inventory. You must maintain accurate stock levels in your dashboard. Accepting orders for out-of-stock items without fulfilling them within the stated handling time may result in account suspension.

4.4 Product Images. You must own or have the rights to use all images submitted. Images must accurately represent the product and may not include watermarks from competing platforms.

4.5 FITR Approval. FITR reserves the right to review, edit, reject, or remove any product listing that violates this Agreement, FITR's content policies, or applicable law — at our sole discretion and without prior notice.

4.6 Listing Suspension. Listings may be suspended due to: customer complaints, quality issues, legal concerns, inaccurate descriptions, or policy violations. We will notify you by email in most cases.

5. Prohibited Items & Conduct

The following are strictly prohibited on the FITR platform:

  • Counterfeit, knockoff, or unlicensed products
  • Products containing controlled substances, illegal stimulants, or banned sports performance drugs
  • Products making false or unsubstantiated health claims (FDA-regulated claims without approval)
  • Weapons, hazardous materials, or items that pose a safety risk
  • Products that infringe on any third party's intellectual property rights
  • Adult content or products
  • Listings that deceive or mislead consumers about the nature, origin, or quality of a product
  • Manipulating reviews, ratings, or search results
  • Using FITR's platform to solicit customers to purchase directly from you outside the platform
  • Sharing another brand's confidential business information obtained through FITR

Violation of this section may result in immediate account termination without refund, and we reserve the right to pursue legal action for damages.

6. Fulfillment & Shipping

6.1 Your Responsibility. You are solely responsible for fulfilling all orders placed through FITR. FITR is a marketplace platform — we do not handle inventory, warehousing, packing, or shipping on your behalf.

6.2 Handling Time. You must specify your handling time (the number of business days to prepare and ship an order) in your dashboard. Orders must be shipped within the stated handling time. Standard maximum handling time is 5 business days unless otherwise agreed.

6.3 Order Notifications. FITR will notify you of new orders via email to your registered brand account email. You are responsible for monitoring that email for order notifications.

6.4 Tracking. You must provide a valid tracking number for all shipped orders within 24 hours of shipment. Failure to provide tracking may result in automatic refunds to customers.

6.5 Shipping Costs. You are responsible for all shipping costs unless you choose to offer free shipping. Shipping rates shown to customers must reflect your actual shipping costs. You may not use shipping as a profit center to circumvent the commission structure.

6.6 Packaging. Products must be packaged to arrive undamaged. You are responsible for any damage resulting from inadequate packaging.

6.7 Failed Deliveries. If a package is lost or damaged in transit, you are responsible for resolving the issue with the carrier and with the customer — including issuing a replacement or refund as appropriate.

7. Returns & Refunds

7.1 Return Policy. You must honor a minimum 14-day return policy for all products sold through FITR, unless the item is clearly marked as non-returnable (e.g., opened supplements, consumables, or hygiene products). Your return policy must be clearly stated in your product listings.

7.2 Defective Items. You must accept returns for any item that arrives defective, damaged, or not as described, regardless of your stated return policy.

7.3 Return Shipping. For returns due to your error (wrong item, defective product, not as described), you must cover return shipping costs. For buyer's remorse returns, you may require the buyer to cover return shipping.

7.4 Refund Processing. Upon receiving a returned item, you must process the refund within 3 business days. Refunds are processed through FITR and deducted from your next payout cycle. The 12% commission is refunded to you when a full refund is issued.

7.5 Chargebacks. If a customer initiates a chargeback with their bank, FITR will notify you and may request evidence to contest it. You are responsible for providing shipment and delivery confirmation. Chargeback fees assessed by payment processors may be passed on to the seller if the dispute is lost and the seller was at fault.

8.3 Currency. All transactions are conducted in USD. You are responsible for any currency conversion fees if your bank account is denominated in another currency.
